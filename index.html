Ways to grow

Small habits that can protect margins

📒 Record purchase cost
Include delivery, discounts, GST treatment and wastage consistently.
🔎 Compare like-for-like
Compare same brand, pack size, quality and credit terms—not just headline price.
📦 Track fast movers
Review actual sales weekly before increasing stock levels.
💰 Separate cash from profit
Inventory value is not profit. Record sales, expenses and outstanding credit separately.
